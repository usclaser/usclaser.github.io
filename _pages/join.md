## Join LASER

We appreciate your interest in the lab! If your research interests align with the group please get in touch using the instructions below (current emphasis on RL/MPC, perception-aware planning, mechanical design/hardware). **We are currently recruiting only a very limited number of Ph.D. students for fall 2027** and research-based M.S./undergrad students already at USC on a rolling basis (see below). We are an interdisciplinary team, and motivated students with backgrounds in robotics, aerospace/mechanical engineering, computer science, controls, and related fields are encouraged to apply.


### Prospective Ph.D. Students
We are always excited to have motivated researchers join our group:

1. Please indicate your interest on your application materials. Submit an official application to [USC Viterbi's Ph.D. program](https://viterbigradadmission.usc.edu/doctoral/how-to-apply-phd/) (ASTE/AME/ECE) by Dec-15 for fall admissions.
2. Make sure you select Prof. Albee as a faculty member of interest.
3. If you would like to email Prof. Albee directly, please send your CV and unofficial transcript with a brief description of your specific interest in the lab with subject line `[LASER-PHD-APPLY]`.
- Interviews begin in Dec/Jan, and USC fellowship recommendations are made by ~Feb, followed by RA offers in ~Mar.
- Spring admissions are rare, but possible. Contact Prof. Albee if you have a special circumstance.

### M.S./Undergrad Students @ USC
We typically recruit at the start of the fall and spring semesters, though occasionally have rolling openings. We typically ask our M.S./undergrad students to dedicate a minimum of 10 hours/week on LASER research so that we can work on a substantial project together. Typical pathways are research-for-credit, research volunteers, and USC's [CURVE](https://viterbiundergrad.usc.edu/research/curve/) and [PURF](https://academicprograms.usc.edu/services/undergraduate-research-and-experiential-learning/usc-provosts-undergraduate-research-fellowships/) fellowship programs (USC undergrad only).

If you are already at USC, please submit [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeCkiDuygLSzrlOHHs24YShlH5j2p1ZsdLDkFwKGhh3CgZxKQ/viewform?usp=dialog), which we use for all of our M.S./undergrad recruiting. We do not currently have positions for M.S./undergrads not at USC. We will reach out to you if we have a research rotation position available and decide to interview you.

You will need:

- Your CV/resume
- Unofficial transcripts
- Some brief descriptions of your interest and fit with the lab:
  - Any projects/papers that strike your interest.
  - Your prior experience and research background.

### Postdocs
We are not currently actively searching for postdocs, but if you think there is an exceptional fit please reach out with:

- A brief summary of one or two research projects we could work on together.
- One or two of your papers you are most excited about.
- Contact information for two to three references.

(*Due to the volume of mail we unfortunately might not be able to respond to all application-related emails and applications individually.)*
